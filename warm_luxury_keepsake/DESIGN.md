## Brand & Style
The visual identity captures the tactile grace, tenderness, and reverence of heirloom gifting for life’s first milestones. It caters to discerning parents, generous relatives, and celebratory gift-givers seeking elevated, thoughtful presentation for baby showers, newborn arrivals, Eid, and foundational birthdays.

The design movement combines **warm editorial minimalism** with **tactile unboxing luxury**. The interface rejects clinical e-commerce tropes in favor of an artisanal boutique presence: generous negative space, gentle transitions, cashmere-soft surfaces, and restrained champagne accents that evoke gilded ribbons and fine stationery.

## Colors
The palette balances serene nursery heritage with sophisticated gift packaging:

- **Primary (`#8C6A58` - Cashmere Rose / Dusty Earth):** Used for primary conversion points, elevated titles, and focal points. Offers warmth and authority without starkness.
- **Secondary (`#738676` - Heirloom Sage):** A calming botanical green for badges, curation indicators, and trust-centered micro-moments.
- **Tertiary (`#CBB592` - Champagne Gilt):** A delicate metallic gold used sparingly for celebratory ribbons, foils, delicate micro-borders, and rating stars.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Ivory Alabaster):** The base background surface, avoiding harsh pure whites to provide a physical rag-paper warmth.
- **Surface Elevation:** Layered tones shift subtly through soft beige (`#F3EFEA`) and warm biscuit (`#E9E3DA`) to create gentle architectural contrast.
- **Text Hierarchy:** High-contrast headings and body copy rely on deep warm umber (`#2D2621`) rather than pure black, maintaining soft editorial contrast and readability.

## Typography
Typographic scale mirrors high-end editorial publications and bespoke stationery:

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Handled exclusively by `Playfair Display`. It provides lyrical cadence, high-contrast strokes, and warmth that validates the keepsake value of the brand.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Driven by `Plus Jakarta Sans`, selected for its open counters, humanist friendliness, and pristine legibility across high-density checkout and customizer forms.
- **Micro-copy & Uppercase Labels:** Set in tracked uppercase `label-caps` for product tags (e.g., "100% ORGANIC PIMA COTTON", "BESPOKE EMBROIDERY") to impart quiet luxury without aggression.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a fixed-width max container (`1280px`) nested in an expansive field of ivory whitespace to preserve boutique intimacy.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with generous `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` canvas margins. Product and curation modules breathe freely with section-to-section gaps of `5rem` to `7rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Side-by-side gift box configurators collapse gracefully into tabbed stacks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` safe outer margins. Multi-item package builders leverage full-bleed horizontal scroll carousels with peek hints.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical luxury packaging layers—vellum sheets, rigid linen boxes, and soft cotton batting:

- **Cashmere Ambient Shadow:** Shadows never use grey or black. They are cast using a warm umber tone at extremely soft diffusion:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 6px 20px -4px rgba(78, 62, 53, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(78, 62, 53, 0.03)`
  - *Elevated Box / Modal:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(78, 62, 53, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(78, 62, 53, 0.04)`
- **Vellum Overlays:** Backdrops use `#FAF7F2` with 80% opacity paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` for delicate, frosted transition states.
- **Gold-Lined Keylines:** Subtly demarcate premium card edges using hair-thin 1px borders colored in `rgba(203, 181, 146, 0.35)` or soft oatmeal `rgba(140, 106, 88, 0.1)`.

## Shapes
Geometry emphasizes softness, comfort, and heirloom craftsmanship. The primary radius is set to `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`), communicating gentle protection appropriate for babyhood.

- **Cards, Gift Boxes & Paneling:** Use `rounded-2xl` (`1rem`) to resemble rounded rigid gift box lids.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Use soft rounded pills (`9999px`) or `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem`), eliminating sharp edges across every interactive touchpoint.
- **Media Containers:** Product imagery carries an inner hairline border radius that mirrors outer parent containers for balanced, nested symmetry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Gilded Ribbon):** Filled with `#8C6A58` with crisp white or warm alabaster text (`label-md`). Hover elevates by `-1px` with a deepening glow tinted by warm gold.
- **Secondary (Boutique Linen):** Background of `#F3EFEA` with a 1px border of `rgba(140, 106, 88, 0.2)` and `#2D2621` text.
- **Tertiary (Subtle Gilt):** Borderless with `#8C6A58` text and a subtle, expanding champagne gold underline on hover.

### Cards & Packaging Tiles
- **Base Style:** Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle `rounded-2xl` corners, cashmere drop shadows, and a 1px border in `#E9E3DA`.
- **Gift Box Configurator Card:** Features high-resolution imagery, subtle fabric swatches, and celebratory tags (e.g., "Hand-Embroidered", "100% Organic").

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Fields:** Background of `#FFFFFF`, rounded to `0.75rem`, enclosed in a 1px soft oatmeal border (`#DED7CD`).
- **Focus State:** Transitions to a glowing 1.5px border of `#CBB592` with zero harsh browser outlines.
- **Typography:** Uses `body-md` in `#2D2621` with placeholder text in soft taupe (`#A29B93`).

### Selection Controls (Radio, Checkboxes & Swatches)
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Softly rounded boxes and circular rings bordered in `#CBB592`. When active, filled with `#8C6A58` with white interior marks.
- **Fabric & Color Swatches:** Rendered as clean circular discs surrounded by a 2px offset border in Champagne Gilt when active.

### Chips & Occasion Badges
- **Badges:** Soft pill shapes with generous padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-md` horizontal).
- **Palettes:** Distinct occasion palettes: Sage Green background (`#EDF1EE`) with `#4E5D50` text for newborns; Soft Pink (`#F9EFF0`) for baby showers; Soft Gold (`#F8F4EC`) with `#7B6645` for Eid & milestone birthdays.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Gift Note / Calligraphy Card Previewer:** A textured ivory card container rendered in Playfair Display italic script, complete with virtual wax seal toggles and font selection.
- **Ribbon & Packaging Selector:** A tactile unboxing preview allowing users to cycle between satin ribbon finishes, stamped gold foils, and custom tissue paper wraps.
