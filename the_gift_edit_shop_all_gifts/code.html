Frequently Asked Questions

Need special coordination for an upcoming birthday or celebration? We make thoughtful gifting entirely seamless and worry-free.

Can I send the gift directly to the recipient? expand_more
Yes, absolutely! Over 80% of our orders are shipped straight to the recipient's doorstep. We conceal all price tags and paper invoices, ensuring the box arrives as a pure surprise with your personalized wax-sealed note on top.
What payment methods do you accept in Pakistan? expand_more
We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan for all catalog gift boxes. If you are sending the gift directly to a friend, we accept Direct Bank Transfer via Raast Instant Pay or IBAN so you can pay seamlessly beforehand.
How long does delivery take? expand_more
Boxes ordered before 2:00 PM are packed and dispatched the very same day. Deliveries within Lahore typically arrive within 24 hours, while Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and Faisalabad arrive in 2 to 3 business days via premier couriers.
Will fragile items like candles and glassware arrive safely? expand_more
Every keepsake box is cushioned internally with high-density crinkle paper, and the entire outer rigid box is enveloped inside corrugated shock-absorbent shipping cartons. In the rare event of transit damage, we issue a prompt replacement immediately.
